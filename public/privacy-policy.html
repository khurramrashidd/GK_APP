Privacy Policy — GK Quiz App

Last updated: August 27, 2026

GK Quiz App ("the App") is a general knowledge quiz application for competitive exam preparation. This policy explains what information the App collects, why, and how it is used.

Information you provide directly

When you sign in and set up your profile, we collect:

  • Required: your name and state
  • Optional: date of birth, mobile number, city, pincode, and gender
  • Your email address and display name (from Google Sign-In or the email you register with)

You may leave any optional field blank. The App remains fully usable without them.

Information collected automatically

  • Your quiz activity — which questions you answer, whether correct, and your cumulative score
  • Basic authentication data managed by Firebase Authentication (Google Sign-In or email/password)

AI-generated explanations (Gemini)

If you tap "Explain with AI" on a quiz question, the question text and its answer options are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate a plain-language explanation. This explanation is then stored and reused for all users who view that same question, so most questions never need a second AI request. No personal profile information (your name, contact details, etc.) is included in this request — only the quiz question content itself.

How your information is used

  • To create and maintain your account and quiz progress
  • To personalize your dashboard and track your score
  • To generate AI explanations for quiz questions, as described above

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising. The App does not display third-party ads.

Where your data is stored

All data is stored on Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication), a service operated by Google under its own security and privacy standards. Quiz questions are also cached locally on your device so the App works offline; this local cache contains no personal profile data, only quiz content.

Your choices

  • Optional profile fields can be left blank or edited any time from your dashboard.
  • You can sign out at any time from the profile screen.
  • To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact us using the details below.

Children's privacy

The App is intended for competitive-exam aspirants and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
